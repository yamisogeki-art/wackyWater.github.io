From download to delivery

Set up takes a few minutes, and you only do it once.

  1. Register

    Add your name, email and a password.

  2. Verify your ID

    Photograph a valid ID with your face visible.

  3. Take two selfies

    One neutral and one smiling, to match your ID.

  4. Order a refill

    Orders open once your account is approved.

  5. Track and receive

    Follow the driver, then check your delivery photo.

Why we check your ID

Matching your selfies to your ID keeps every account tied to a real customer. If a check needs another look, the app shows your status and tells you what to resubmit.

Get Wacky Water on your Android phone

Download the file, open it, and install. Your phone may ask for permission the first time.

Download APK for Android
  1. Tap Download APK for Android.
  2. If your browser warns you, tap Download anyway.
  3. Open the downloaded WackyWater.apk file.
  4. If asked, turn on Allow from this source in Android Settings.
  5. Tap Install, then open Wacky Water.
